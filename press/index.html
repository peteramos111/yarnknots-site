Short description

Yarn Knots is a cozy puzzle game about untangling yarn: drag the balls until no strings cross, in a sunlit living room, while Biscuit the cat watches, judges and occasionally swats.

Long description

Every level starts as a tangle of yarn spread across a rug. Strings that cross glow red; drag the yarn balls until every string runs free. It is simple to learn and quietly satisfying to finish.

On the sofa sits Biscuit, a fluffy cream cat with green eyes and a lot of opinions. He breathes, blinks, yawns and glances around the room, and every so often he stares at a ball, crouches, and swats it across the rug. Grab the ball first and he loses interest. If he gets it, undoing his swat is always free. His running commentary is dry, adult cat-owner humour: “Biscuit would like to help. Biscuit will not be helping.”

Players earn yarn for each level and climb the rankings past rival cats like Mochi, Tofu and Noodle. Hints, undo and restart are there when you need them, with short optional videos to earn more.

Features

  • Hand-tuned, always-solvable tangles with a gentle difficulty curve
  • Biscuit, a lifelike animated cat who watches, yawns, glances around and swats
  • Dry, grown-up cat-owner humour in every line
  • A warm, photo-real living-room setting with real-looking wool
  • Rankings by yarn, with a bonus for reaching #1
  • Hints, undo and restart, never forced, always optional
  • A free demo that plays right in the browser, no download or account
