Short description

Yarn Knots is a cozy puzzle game about untangling yarn: drag the yarn balls until no strings cross, while a very helpful, and sometimes mischievous, cat watches.

Long description

Every level starts as a tangle of yarn. Red knots show where strings cross; drag the balls until every string runs free and the knots pop with a puff of fluff. A cat peeks up from the bottom of the board, follows every move, and every so often reaches out to swat a ball, sometimes helpfully and sometimes not.

Players unlock five cats with their own personalities, dress them up in hats, take on a new Daily Tangle every day, keep streaks going for bonus yarn, and climb a worldwide leaderboard.

Features

  • Hundreds of hand-tuned, always-solvable tangles with a gentle difficulty curve
  • Five cats to unlock: Biscuit, Sir Pounce, Miso, Pillow and Pepper
  • A new Daily Tangle every day, with streak rewards
  • A worldwide leaderboard with a podium for the top three
  • Hats and yarn color themes to collect
