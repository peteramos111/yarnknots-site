Playtest

Help us test the game. Biscuit will be watching.

About 10–15 minutes · works best on your phone · no sign-up needed. Honest first impressions are exactly what we need: “this part was boring” helps more than “it’s great!”

  1. Open the demo in a new tabyarnknots.com/play. Please don’t read any tips first. We want to see if the game explains itself.
  2. Play for about 10 minutesTry to get through all 5 levels. Keep an eye on Biscuit on the sofa. If you get stuck, try Hint, Back or Restart, or give up. That tells us something too.
  3. Come back here and answer the questions belowIt takes about 3 minutes. Skip anything you don’t have an opinion on.
What did you play on?
Overall, how much did you enjoy it?
How was the difficulty?
Biscuit himself: how did he come across?
Biscuit swatting balls out of place: fun or annoying?
Biscuit’s comments (“Bold move.”, “Biscuit wanted to help… he didn’t.”)
Hint, Back and Restart run out, and a short video gets you more. How does that feel?
If the full game were free on your phone (with optional ads), would you download it?

We’ll also include a short summary of your demo progress in this browser (current level, best times, yarn, and hints, undos and restarts left) so we can see how far people get.

Please only send feedback if you’re 14 or older. We use your answers only to improve the game. By sending, you agree to our terms; see our privacy policy for how feedback is handled.

Thank you. Biscuit is, briefly, impressed.

Your feedback goes straight to the person making the game, and it really does shape what happens next.
