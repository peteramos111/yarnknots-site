About 10–15 minutes · works best on your phone · no sign-up needed

Yarn Knots is a cozy puzzle game: drag the yarn balls until no strings cross, while a cat watches, cheers and now and then swats a ball out of place. It isn’t finished yet, so your honest first impressions are exactly what we need. “This part was boring” is more useful to us than “it’s great!”

  1. Open the demo in a new tabyarnknots.com/play. Please don’t read any tips first. We want to see if the game explains itself.
  2. Play for about 10 minutesTry to get through at least 5 levels (all 10 if you’re enjoying it), and try the Daily Tangle once. If you get stuck, it’s fine to use Cat help or give up. That tells us something too.
  3. Come back to this page and answer the questions belowIt takes about 3 minutes. Skip anything you don’t have an opinion on.
What did you play on?
Overall, how much did you enjoy it?
How was the difficulty?
The cat swatting balls out of place: fun or annoying?
Sounds and the cheering voice (“Purr-fect!”, meows, purring)
If the full game were free on your phone (with ads, no purchases), would you download it?
