One rule. Many knots. One cat.

Every tangle has the same goal: no crossing strings. Crossings glow red, so you always know what’s still in a knot.

Drag until it’s calm

Move the yarn balls around the rug until every string runs free. Easy to start, quietly satisfying to finish.

A cat with opinions

Biscuit stares, yawns, glances around, and every so often reaches up and swats a ball. Undoing his swat is always free.

Yarn, rankings, bragging

Earn yarn for every level and climb past Mochi, Tofu and the rest. Hints, undo and restart are there when you need them.
