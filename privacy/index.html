Privacy policy

We collect as little as possible.

Plain-language answers to what we keep, why, and how to reach us.

Effective October 29, 2026

The short version: the website and the free demo collect as little as possible. Your game progress stays on your own device, unless you choose to send it to us with playtest feedback. We only get your email address if you give it to us (launch news signup, contact form or playtest feedback), and we never sell your information.

This policy explains how Gudthingz, a sole proprietorship based in Ottawa, Ontario, Canada (“Yarn Knots”, “we”, “us”), handles information when you visit yarnknots.com or play the free browser demo of Yarn Knots. The Yarn Knots mobile app will have its own privacy policy, published here before it launches.

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies on this website. The demo shows no real ads: its “watch a video” option plays a short placeholder, and no ad network is used.

How we use it

  • To send launch news to people who asked for it. Every email includes a one-click unsubscribe link. Our emails may contain a small invisible image and tracked links that tell Sender, our email service, whether an email was opened and which links were clicked. We use this only to see whether our news is useful, never to build advertising profiles.
  • To answer messages you send us, and to improve the game using playtest feedback you choose to send.
  • To improve the website and the game, using visit statistics that don’t identify you.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising profiles.

Legal basis (EEA and UK visitors)

We rely on your consent for launch emails (you tick a box to sign up, and you can withdraw at any time), on our legitimate interest in answering messages and reading feedback and understanding site usage, and on legal obligations where they apply.

Service providers

We use a small number of trusted providers to run the site: Sender (email signups), Formspree (contact and playtest forms), GoatCounter (visit statistics), GitHub Pages (website hosting) and Google Fonts (typefaces). Google Fonts receives your IP address when it delivers the typefaces to your browser. They process information only to provide their service to us. Some of them are located outside your country, including in the United States and the European Union, so your information may be processed there under appropriate safeguards.

How long we keep it

  • Launch-news emails: until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete your address, or until 12 months after the app launches, whichever comes first.
  • Contact messages and playtest feedback: up to 24 months, then deleted.
  • Visit statistics: kept in summary form; they don’t identify you.
  • Demo progress: stays in your browser until you clear your browser data.

Your choices and rights

You can unsubscribe from emails at any time using the link in any email. Depending on where you live (including the EEA, UK, Canada and California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal information, to object to or restrict its use, and to withdraw consent. To make a request, email hello@yarnknots.com. We will respond within 30 days. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

The website and demo are intended for a general audience and are not directed at children under 14. Please only sign up for launch news, use the contact form or send playtest feedback if you are 14 or older. Before the launch news signup appears, we ask for your birth year. If it shows you are under 14, the signup is refused and your email is not collected. Playing the demo itself does not send us any personal information.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14. If we learn that we have received personal information from a child under 14, for example through an email signup, a contact message or playtest feedback, we will delete it promptly.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has sent us information, email hello@yarnknots.com and we will delete it. We will only ask for what we need to find it, such as the email address that was used.

Security

The website is served over an encrypted connection (HTTPS), and we limit who can access the information we hold. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we will also notify people on our email list.
